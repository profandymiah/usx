How it works

From sign-up to standing out

1

Join USx

Follow the WhatsApp channel and register with USx, so you hear about every opportunity as soon as it opens.

2

Apply for what suits you

Each opportunity has its own call-out and a short sign-up form. Pick the roles and days that fit around your studies.

3

Take part and build your record

Work real events, meet professionals and create content for your portfolio. Your involvement is recorded, so we can recognise it and invite you to more.
