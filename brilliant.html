The role

What you'll do

  • ◎
    CaptureShoot photos, short videos and quick interviews with speakers, exhibitors, teachers and young people across the festival.
  • ✂
    CreateTurn what you capture into reels, stories, carousels and short posts while the day is still happening.
  • ↗
    DistributePublish and share content across festival and Salford channels, keeping the live feed moving from first doors to final session.
In return

What you get

Full festival accessA day inside the UK's largest STEAM education festival.
Published workContent that goes out on live channels, for your portfolio and CV.
A media partner roleWork as part of Salford's team as the festival's Official Media Partner.
New connectionsMeet educators, STEAM organisations, industry partners and the festival team.
Is this for you?

Who it suits

Students who love making content, from any course: media, journalism, film, photography, marketing, science communication, design or STEM. If you already post, edit or shoot on your phone, you've got the skills. Bring examples of your work if you have them.
